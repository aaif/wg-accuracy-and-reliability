# [Report] Accuracy & Reliability – October 2026

**Working Group:** WG Accuracy & Reliability

**Reporting period:** October 2026

**Chair(s):** Jordan Augé (Cisco) & Casper Nielsen (Diagrid)

**Date submitted:** 2026-11-10

---

## Summary

In October, the working group wrapped up the gap-analysis survey, which is now under review ahead of wider dissemination, and submitted its six taxonomy contributions. It also held its community virtual summit, which surfaced a link with Princeton's work on agent reliability and new gaps to feed into Deliverable 2.

## Progress Against Objectives

- **Survey:** wrapped up the survey; it is now under review before further dissemination.
- **Taxonomy / terminology:** submitted 6 PRs, one for each of the 6 definitions identified as contributions needed from the ARWG: [Accuracy](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/101), [Reliability](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/102), [Robustness](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/103), [Safety](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/104), [Recoverability](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/106), [Infrastructure](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/105).
- **Virtual summit:** held the [Accuracy & Reliability of AI Agents Virtual Summit](https://home.mlops.community/public/events/accuracy-and-reliability-of-ai-agents-virtual-summit-77nje0s530), organized by Sanjeev. Sessions covered:
  - *Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability*: agent reliability as a distinct axis from capability; recent model releases have mostly improved capability, not reliability.
  - *Missing Abstractions for High-stakes Agentic Systems*: lessons from building large-scale, customer-facing agentic systems.
  - *Silent Failures, Loud Consequences*: hidden cognitive failures compounding in multi-agent systems, and how to detect and mitigate them.
  - *Crash-Proof Agents*: durable execution as a reliability layer (resume, safe retry, avoiding repeated side effects).
  - Closing open discussion on the biggest reliability challenges and open questions.
- **Contributions to the failure taxonomy:** identified contributions from Cisco.
- **Princeton contact:** established contact with the authors of [*Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666), whose work provides structural grounding for the WG's own.
- **Gap analysis (Deliverable 2):** two other summit talks point to gaps we want to address: durable execution, and a use-case-driven talk on missing abstractions for high-stakes systems.
