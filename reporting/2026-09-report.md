# [Report] Accuracy & Reliability – September 2026

**Working Group:** WG Accuracy & Reliability

**Reporting period:** September 2026

**Chair(s):** Jordan Augé (Cisco) & Casper Nielsen (Diagrid)

**Date submitted:** 2026-10-02

---

## Summary

In September, the working group advanced its taxonomy and gap-analysis deliverables, finalized and merged the survey, and planned outreach to broaden participation. Members also used AGNTCon discussions and presentations to inform the gap analysis and began organizing a community virtual summit on AI agent accuracy and reliability.

## Progress Against Objectives

- Finalized and merged the gap-analysis survey, incorporating feedback and preparing for outreach. The group discussed survey promotion through foundation and community channels, conference materials, and a possible early-results discussion.
- Advanced the terminology deliverable with Matthew's taxonomy browser, which supports proposing and reviewing definitions, including tracking AI-assisted contributions. Members were encouraged to contribute core terms such as accuracy and reliability; biweekly review and merging were proposed.
- Developed the Deliverable 2 gap-analysis approach around agent lifecycle stages and common patterns, with concrete use cases, current tools, gaps, and potential solutions. AgentCon presentations and discussions informed this work; members were asked to add use cases and gaps, including user trust and perceived accuracy.
- Planned a community virtual summit to share practical experience and promote participation. The event is described as covering agent evaluation, benchmarks, reliability, verification, failure analysis, and deployment lessons, with a call for 10- or 20-minute speaker proposals: [Accuracy & Reliability of AI Agents Virtual Summit](https://home.mlops.community/public/events/accuracy-and-reliability-of-ai-agents-virtual-summit-77nje0s530).
- Identified opportunities to coordinate with other working groups on observability, terminology, workflow integration, and related cross-cutting challenges.

## Blockers and Risks

- Participation and feedback remain important risks for both deliverables and survey reach. The group is pursuing conference, mailing-list, and community-event outreach.
- Reviewing AI-assisted contributions can be difficult when contributors do not explain or understand generated content; the group identified a need for contribution guidance.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

None this month.

## Next Month's Focus

1. Promote the survey and virtual summit, confirm event logistics and speakers, and encourage broad participation.
2. Gather concrete use cases and gaps for Deliverable 2, using AgentCon learnings and member experience to shape the lifecycle and pattern-based analysis.
3. Grow the terminology contribution and review cycle, prioritizing definitions of accuracy and reliability and clarifying expectations for AI-assisted contributions.
