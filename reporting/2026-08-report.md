## Blockers and Risks

- **Seasonal participation constraints**
  - Summer period and PTOs reduced review throughput and writing bandwidth.
- **Review concentration risk**
  - Discussion is progressing, but writing/review load remains concentrated in a small contributor subset.
  - In practical terms, "low engagement" this month means active discussion in meetings/threads but a limited number of contributors making sustained writing/review edits across deliverable PRs; involvement appears to be slowly increasing with new active contributors during summer.
- **D2 prioritization evidence still thin**
  - Persona/use-case/pain-point framing exists, but comparative evidence remains limited until survey feedback lands.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

- Confirm that the survey-review-first approach is sufficient to prioritize D2 next steps, and that TC can support survey reach-out beyond ARWG channels once [PR #9](https://github.com/aaif/wg-accuracy-and-reliability/pull/9) is finalized.

## Next Month's Focus

1. Close survey review round and merge PR #9 after meeting discussion and deadline.
2. Launch focused D1 PR #8 review on the first terminology subset for contribution to the shared Taxonomy & Landscape workstream.
3. Prepare survey rollout and collection plan; synthesize initial feedback only if enough responses are received in time.
